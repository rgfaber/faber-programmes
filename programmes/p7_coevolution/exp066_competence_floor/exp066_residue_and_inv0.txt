-- C. THE 13 PHASE 1 SEEDS' INV AT CHECKPOINT 0 --

Answers the DESIGN gate's RC-3. INV(C) = the number of band-decisive
cyclic triples of the panel matrix that contain C, which is Null C's
statistic evaluated on the panel itself.

LOWER BOUND, and why: only the 190 champion-versus-champion cells of the
25-member phase 1 panel exist on disk (SC3a forces the panel to reproduce
them cell for cell). The 5 scripted rungs can only ADD triples. The
gunless-leg exclusion removes triples through sitting_duck or spinner,
neither of which is in this matrix, so it removes nothing here.

n_C   = members whose edge to C is band-decisive
k_C   = how many of those C beats
D_C   = band-decisive pairs inside that sub-panel
INV   = cyclic triples containing C
E_INV = D_C * k_C * (n_C - k_C) / (n_C * (n_C - 1)), Null C's closed form,
        evaluated exactly, no permutation sampled, no RNG used
