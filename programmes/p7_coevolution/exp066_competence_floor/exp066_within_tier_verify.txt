-- E. THE ORIENTATION NULL'S CLOSED FORM, NO RNG NEEDED --

Under the null the decisive set is fixed and each decisive edge's
direction is a fair coin, so a cyclable triple is cyclic with
probability 2/8 and contributes 2*P(fwd)+1*P(bwd) = 3/8 to the ordered
count. Hence E[cycles] = C/4 and E[ordered] = 3C/8 with C the number of
cyclable triples. Two triples share at most one edge, and conditioning
on that edge's direction leaves each triple cyclic with probability 1/4
either way, so the cycle indicators are pairwise uncorrelated and
sd[cycles] = sqrt(3C/16) exactly.
