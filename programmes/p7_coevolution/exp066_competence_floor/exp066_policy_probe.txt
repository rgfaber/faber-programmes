Exploratory, post hoc, NOT pre-registered, SIGNS NOTHING.
Engine pin a5e8bcfc5646827e9be49a9629f8a6a9678c814b, golden match hash re-checked by the runner's own gates.
Every champion is read from exp066_champions_{s,l,d}.eterm in this directory.
The pilot encoder and the match loop are the runner's OWN compiled code, reached through an export_all view built from its beam's debug_info.
held-out starts requested = 80, used = 80, both seats always, so matches per opponent = 160

COLUMNS
  W            win rate (alive and opponent dead), the feed's own rule
  shots/hits   gun_heat zero-to-nonzero transitions, and of those the ones whose bullet reaches the victim inside HIT_R AND for which the engine credited the shooter damage on that same turn (see section A)
  cf_hit       the SAME bullet against a GHOST victim that holds the velocity it had when the shot left the barrel
  weave        missed the real champion, hit the ghost: a shot the champion's change of velocity defeated
  blind        missed both: a shot already aimed wrong when it left the barrel
  latmiss      mean absolute lateral miss at closest approach, whole units, against HIT_R = 20
  devlat       of that, the part explained by the victim departing from its fire-time velocity
  estlat       of that, the part explained by the bot's own velocity ESTIMATE being wrong at fire time
  fresh        share of turns on which the bot held a scan from THIS turn, which is its precondition for firing at all
  velzero      share of turns on which the bot's enemy_vel/1 returned standing still (never seen twice, or a scan gap over 8)
  bodyflip     sign changes per 100 turns in the COMMANDED turn_body
  vperp        mean own speed PERPENDICULAR to the line of sight, whole units per turn: the quantity the lead solver extrapolates
  mypow/oppow  mean commanded shot power, in tenths. The floor bot's own budget rule drops it from 30 to 20 beyond 150 units of range and to 10 beyond 350, and damage per hit is 16, 10 and 4 units respectively
  ram          turns per match with the tanks overlapping. Ram contact costs BOTH tanks 0.6 energy a turn and robo_sim credits it to nobody, so it never appears in dealt or taken
  latflip      sign changes per 100 turns in cross(line of sight, own velocity): reversals of the quantity the lead solver extrapolates
  range        mean tank separation over the match, whole units

SELF-CHECK. bullets counted as hitting by this module's own walk must equal the engine's damage credits; dealt/taken are read from #tank.damage_dealt and printed beside them.
