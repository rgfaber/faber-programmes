-- B. FIX B: PH-GEN, A NEW POST-HOC THREE-SPLIT GENERALISATION DIAGNOSTIC --

PH-GEN IS NOT AN IF CODE AND IS NOT A REPAIR OF IF-8. Read this first.

WHAT IF-8 WAS. 'IF-8 MEMORISATION' fires when train_w >= B and held-out W <
B, with B = 0.5748. train_w reads 1.0000 for 40 of 40 champions, and that is
forced by the design rather than observed: the train split is 6 starts times
2 seats = 12 matches, one step is 1/12 = 0.0833, and ladder/3 clears a rung
only when EVERY match of it has a positive margin over exactly those same 12
matches. Any champion whose archived fitness cleared the gun rung therefore
had 12 positive margins there, and in every case 12 wins. The left conjunct
is vacuously true everywhere, so IF-8 collapses to 'the median champion is
below B', which is the negation of half the CLEARED test and carries no
train-to-held-out contrast at all.

THAT STANDS. IF-8 was untestable with this instrument, its quiet state in the
feed says nothing about memorisation, and PH-GEN does not change that. IF-8 is
not modified, not rescored and not reinterpreted anywhere in this record.

WHAT PH-GEN IS. A NEW diagnostic, post hoc, unregistered, first computed
2026-07-30. It reads the honest three-way split of one deterministic 120-start
generator:
  train        starts   1..6    6 starts, 12 matches   the optimiser saw these
  held out     starts   7..86  80 starts, 160 matches  the gate lives here
  calibration  starts  87..116 30 starts, 60 matches   built arm C, measured
                                                       NO champion
Disjoint by index construction and asserted by gate_starts/0. Starts 117..120
are generated and deliberately unused.

HOW CLEAN THE THIRD SET ACTUALLY IS, stated exactly. During the RUN the
calibration starts scored arm C and nothing else: no evolution arm and no
champion was ever measured on them, and nothing was ever selected using them.
They have since been READ ONCE, post hoc: exp066_two_attractors_probe.txt
section K reports the calibration win rate of all 20 arm S champions, and the
calW column below reproduces it. So for arm S this is a re-read of an already
published post-hoc measurement, not a first look, and it is not a held-back
test set in the strict sense any more. For arms L and D it is a first look.
Either way nothing was selected on it, which is the property that matters.

TWO QUANTITIES PER SPLIT, both against the floor bot. (a) the win rate, the
same rule the gate uses, draws counting as not beating. (b) the mean of
margin/1 in whole units: the FLOORED margin, which applies the one-bar death
floor on both sides. Floored is what makes two splits comparable and is also
exactly what the fitness reads. THE FEED'S OWN margin COLUMN DOES NOT FLOOR,
so it is not comparable across splits; it appears in section C only, to tie
each row back to the feed.

REPLAY, NOT A RE-RUN. Scoring an archived champion on the calibration starts
is heldout/3 -> duel/3 -> play_seat/5 -> play/2 -> robo_sim. No optimiser is
reachable from that path. Confirmed by reading the code, and stated here.
