  is the probability that a matrix DRAWN FROM Null A exceeds the maximum of
  200 further draws from Null A. The event the threshold actually counts is
  a PHASE 1 ARCHIVE MATRIX exceeding it, and the only real matrix available
  exceeds it at every band by a wide margin while containing no coevolution
  at all. E6 gives the arithmetic for what the threshold would have to be.

  different document and a different agent's edit.

E6. WHAT THE THRESHOLD WOULD HAVE TO BE, AT WHAT RATE.

  The level to hold is the one 3-of-13 buys at p = 1/201: P(>=3) = 3.39e-5
  per arm. So the question is the smallest k of 13 whose tail is at or
  below that level, at a given per-run rate q.

  q          what q is                              smallest k of 13
  0.00498    the registered rate, a matrix drawn FROM   3
             Null A
  0.05000    Clopper-Pearson one-sided 95% LOWER bound  6
             from 1 exceedance in 1 matrix: solve
             1 - (1 - q) = 0.05, so q >= 0.05
  1.00000    the point estimate from 1 of 1             none

  Read the last row first. At q = 1 no k of 13 holds the level, because
  P(>=13 of 13) = 1. The threshold cannot be repaired by raising k. At the
  95% lower bound the threshold would be 6 of 13 rather than 3, and that
  is the MOST FAVOURABLE reading one exceedance in one matrix allows.

  AND RAISING k IS STILL NOT THE REPAIR. q here is not a Type I rate. It
  is the rate at which this substrate's matrices are non-scalar, which the
  phase 0 matrix says is the default and not the exception. A threshold of
  6 of 13 would require that 6 of 13 phase 1 runs be non-scalar, which is
  a bar this substrate clears without any coevolution. The quantity that
  survives the arithmetic is a COMPARISON against a no-coevolution
  reference of the same shape, and D3 already prints one: the observed
  share of cyclable triples that are cyclic, against the null's share and
  against this matrix's own share. Which reference the pre-registration
  adopts is a design decision and is NOT taken here.

-- WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT --

observed decisiveness structure; whether IF-9's chosen width accepts a
matrix the null is meant to describe; and where a real, largely
ladder-shaped matrix's cycle count sits in the null's own 200 draws.

DOES NOT ESTABLISH. Nothing about coevolution and nothing about phase 1.

phase 0 runs, not 20 checkpoints of one coevolutionary lineage, so its
dependence structure is not an archive matrix's. It calibrates the
instrument; it does not predict what the instrument will read.

NOT A VERDICT. No phase 1 outcome is decided here, no threshold in this
