-- JUDGEMENT: HAS NULL A THE POWER TO SEPARATE A CYCLIC MATRIX
   FROM A TRANSITIVE ONE, ON A MATRIX OF THIS SHAPE? --

IT HAS POWER, AND THE OBSERVED COUNT IS NOT DEEP INSIDE THE
BOOTSTRAP SPREAD. It sits outside the spread by orders of
magnitude. So this is a finding about the DATA, not a confession
that the instrument is blind here.

The closed form settles it with no RNG at all. Summed exactly over
all 1140 triples from the fitted per-edge orientation
probabilities, the expected number of banded 3-cycles in ONE
synthetic matrix is 1.02822896 at band 0.05, 0.04797213 at band 0.10 and
0.00081969 at band 0.15 (DETAIL B). The observed counts are 49, 18 and
12. Markov's inequality needs no distributional assumption and
gives P(a null draw reaches the observed count) =< 0.02098426 at band
0.05, =< 0.00266512 at band 0.10 and =< 0.00006831 at band 0.15. A null whose
expected cycle count is a few hundredths cannot put an observation
of 18 in the middle of its spread.

WHERE THE POWER COMES FROM. It is not bought by starving the null
of decisive edges. The closed form gives the null MORE cyclable
triples than the observed matrix has at bands 0.05 and 0.10
(DETAIL B against the observed 789 and 582), so the null has more
cycle opportunity and still produces almost no cycles. In a
fitted-strength world a band-decisive edge nearly always points
the way the strengths do. The shortfall is orientation, not
opportunity, and the record says the same thing.

THE ONE REAL LIMIT, WHICH IS NOT A LOSS OF POWER. A one-sided test
whose null mass sits on 0 has no RESOLUTION above its maximum: the
bootstrap p saturates at (0+1)/201 = 0.004975 and cannot tell 2
cycles from 18. That is a defect of the p-value as a summary, not
of the null. The raw counts and the closed-form expectation carry
the information the p-value throws away, which is why both belong
in the record.

AND THE POWER CUTS BOTH WAYS, WHICH IS THE CALIBRATION'S POINT. A
test this sharp fires on a matrix with no coevolution in it, so
firing cannot by itself mean coevolutionary cycling. Power against
the scalar-strength model is not power against the hypothesis
phase 1 wants to test. On that the record and this check agree.

-- WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT --

ESTABLISHES. That the calibration's arithmetic is reproducible from an
implementation that shares nothing with it: the fit, the observed
counts on both counters, the IF-9 verdict, the fitted-order violation
counts, the family-wise tail probabilities and the re-derivation
arithmetic. And that the simulated spread the calibration reports sits
where its own closed form says it must, which the calibration never
checked.

DOES NOT ESTABLISH. Nothing about coevolution, nothing about phase 1,
and nothing about whether the pre-registration's conclusions are the
right ones. This file checks numbers. The matrix is 20 independently
evolved champions and a phase 1 archive matrix is not.
