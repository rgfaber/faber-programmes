-- WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH --

ESTABLISHES. The withdrawn mean-centred rule sends a pure transitive
climb to branch 7 for 11 of the 13 seeds and on the median at every
band (D1). The installed exceedance rule sends it to neither branch,
for two independent reasons (D2), and cannot fire anywhere on the whole
prefix-climb family for any champion at any band (D3).

DOES NOT ESTABLISH. That the exceedance leg is REACHABLE by a real
coevolved win pattern. D4 measures the opposite on the only real
patterns available: all 20 coevolution-free champions sit at or below
their own exact mean at every band, so none of them could exceed its own
permutation maximum. That is a lower bound over 190 cells and the
25-member read is pre-registered at protocol step 2, but the direction it
points is that PANEL-VISIBLE and branch 7 may both be hard to reach, and
the pre-registration carries that as a declared limitation on BOTH.

DOES NOT ESTABLISH. Anything about phase 1 archive matrices, which do
not exist. Every champion here is one of 20 independently evolved phase 0
champions, not a checkpoint of a coevolutionary lineage.
